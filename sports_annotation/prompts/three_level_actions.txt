VISUAL ANNOTATION: ACTION EPISODES AND FUNCTIONAL ACTIONS
Use only the supplied timestamped frames. Write concise Chinese descriptions, evidence, and uncertainties; retain the schema's canonical IDs and labels. Output JSON only.

Apply the supplied three-level protocol as the controlling annotation standard. The domain lexicon supplies vocabulary and visual cues, not an expected sequence. The actor count, action count, directions, and repetitions must be inferred from this media. No corrected annotations or earlier model results are supplied.
This pass produces L1 episodes, L2 functional actions, and separate states, phases and keypoints. Do NOT produce L3 primitives in this pass. A continuous chasse bout is one L2; its cycles are repeat_count if countable. A stroke or throw attempt is a discrete instance. Internal backswing, forward swing and follow-through are phase tags, not extra L2 actions. Hand feeding/throwing is annotated when observed for either participant.

For every shot, identify up to two main actors with stable shot-local IDs such as S01-P01. Do not infer identity or role continuity across cuts. Describe appearance and screen position, then establish who actually delivers and who executes each stroke from motion evidence. Role labels cannot replace observation.
For each actor, inspect the sequence before, between and after executions. Include visible approach/recovery motions, intermediate movements and waiting states. Do not force intervals to abut: simultaneous movements can overlap and unresolved gaps remain uncertain. Recovery is a role of the observed movement; it is not automatically chasse. Raised arms alone do not prove a throw. Never add a repeated attempt unless a fresh execution has visible support.
Estimate screen displacement by comparing the same actor's pelvis/trunk and support feet across frames; distinguish screen directions from anatomical left/right. The racket's pointing direction does not establish whole-body displacement. If the viewpoint prevents a decision, state that uncertainty.
Select the narrowest supported stroke label. A lift need not have a high trajectory or a verified deep landing. An upward swing alone may warrant a broader label. Preserve unsuccessful, incomplete and cut-off actions rather than filling the textbook sequence.

Each action and state needs one to three decisive evidence entries using exact timestamps from supplied frames. All times use the clip-global clock. Preserve uncertain/clipped boundaries. Contacts can be time=null when unobserved; do not convert expected contact to precise observed contact. A keypoint may lie at an action endpoint. No OCR, subtitle transcription, speech meaning, sound-source attribution, or inferred hidden biomechanics.
L1 organizes one actor's observed attempt. Do not merge the feeder and striker into one episode. Connect L1/L2 through episode_links with explicit supported overlap intervals. Do not create duplicate aliases of the same action. Include generic visible movements when the lexicon lacks a suitable technical class and mark their vocabulary status.
Use phases only for visible stages of a particular L2; hold states remain separate. Cap each description at two short sentences. List genuine uncertainty rather than suppressing it to satisfy a complete-looking hierarchy.
