AUDIO-ONLY ANNOTATION WITH VERBATIM TRANSCRIPTION
Listen to the supplied original audio using the clip-global clock. No video, subtitles, action labels, expected words or previous annotations are supplied. Annotate only what is audible.

Speech: transcribe the exact spoken words in the original language, including brief counts, repetitions, fillers and unfinished phrases. Do not paraphrase, translate, complete a cut-off sentence or replace the words with a description of speech. Use [听不清] only for genuinely unintelligible spans, preserving intelligible words around them. Each speech event must have a nonempty transcript. An empty list is allowed when there is no speech; never invent speech to satisfy the schema.
Segment utterances at audible pauses or speaker changes, not camera cuts or expected action boundaries. A continuous utterance can span a cut and overlap multiple actions. Short isolated words or counts may be their own utterances. Use V01, V02, etc. only when voices are acoustically distinguishable; otherwise speaker_id=null. Voice IDs do not identify on-screen people. In description, record audible voice characteristics and speaking state (tone, volume, pace), not the meaning of the utterance.

Non-speech: annotate distinguishable transients, friction/squeaks, music and sustained background. Describe acoustic properties without guessing a physical source from context. Keep transcript=null and speaker_id=null for non-speech. Sustained background is a background-layer interval; speech and discrete foreground sounds use foreground. Permit actual overlaps. Do not turn every expected sport contact into a sound event.

Provide independent onset/offset estimates, boundary uncertainty brackets containing the estimates, an audible evidence time, and confidence. Use the valid audio span; do not label padded silence. Timing is model-estimated, not forced alignment. All times are seconds relative to clip start. No minimum event duration. Do not use speech meanings to infer visual actions or temporal links.
IDs: U001, U002, ... for speech; N001, N002, ... for non-speech. Write descriptions and uncertainties in concise Chinese. Return only the supplied JSON schema.
