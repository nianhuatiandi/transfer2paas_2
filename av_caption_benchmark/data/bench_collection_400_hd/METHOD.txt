This collection uses measured output durations: <30 seconds, 30–60 seconds inclusive, and >60 seconds. The final quotas are 240, 120, and 40 clips, with 48, 24, and 8 sports clips respectively. Sports selection requires a visible sport or exercise action in the sampled footage. Source labels are kept for retrieval and can be more specific than the broad content domain.

Both the downloaded input stream and the released MP4 have a short edge of at least 720 pixels. 720p and 1080p indicate encoded frame dimensions. The collection does not infer the camera sensor resolution or recover pre-archive production history. If a clip was resized during collection, the source was downscaled. Source windows and dimensions are in manifest.jsonl. Retained clips report time relative to the previously prepared file. For keyframe cuts the interval starts at the recorded source packet boundary.

Audio was decoded and checked for a non-silent signal. Video passed a full decode while generating frame samples, or was retained from the previously verified collection. The proposal of at most three principal people was screened with a person detector and chronological frame strips. Review sheets can miss very short cuts or occluded subjects. Background people may appear. The separate evidence files contain timestamps, counts, and detection boxes. A small number of sport clips with partial participants were reviewed manually after the detector returned sparse evidence.

The manifest includes available creator, license and upstream metadata. A YouTube URL is included only when present in source metadata. Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons, and local material have distinct acquisition records. Source IDs, hashes and visual fingerprints were checked for repetitions; cross-archive reposts may have incomplete identifiers.
